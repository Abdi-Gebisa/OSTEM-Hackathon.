# OSTEM — Pitch and Demo Notes

## 45-second pitch
Many Ethiopian secondary-school students study Physics without enough opportunities to experiment. OSTEM turns a phone into an interactive Physics learning laboratory. Students can enter their own values, change experimental conditions, watch models respond, inspect measurements and graphs, and compare predictions with scientific equations. The platform also provides an offline Physics tutor, EUEE practice, a formula calculator, lab report builder and short learning challenges. It is designed for mobile devices and low-connectivity environments. Our vision is to make practical Physics learning more accessible, while keeping essential learning free and independent of wallets or payments.

## Questions judges may ask
**Is the tutor generative AI?** No. This submission includes an offline curated/rule-based Physics tutor. A secure generative-AI connection is a future enhancement and is not claimed as active.

**Is the achievement stored on Solana?** Not in this build. The app generates a local SHA-256 proof. An Anchor source prototype is included separately, but it is not built, deployed or verified on Devnet.

**Why Physics only?** The project prioritizes depth, scientific accuracy and usable experiments over shallow coverage of multiple subjects.

**What happens without internet?** After the PWA is first loaded online and its assets are cached, the core static learning experience is designed to work offline.

**How will you measure impact?** Pilot with students and Physics teachers; measure task completion, conceptual pre/post quiz improvement, repeat usage, and whether students can explain how changing variables affects outcomes. Collect only consented, minimal and non-sensitive feedback.
